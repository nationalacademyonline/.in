Your Family's Privacy is Respected

We value your trust. Any details you provide—such as your child’s name, contact numbers, or comments—are used solely by National Academy staff to help your child or respond to your enquiry. We never share your personal information.

What Happens After You Submit?

For admission forms, our friendly office team will call you within 24 to 48 hours to explain school timings, fee structure, and arrange a campus visit. For feedback or concerns, school leadership reviews every message promptly to take action.

Need help filling out any form or have questions?

Visit our campus office in Taraiya or get in touch directly with our administration team.
